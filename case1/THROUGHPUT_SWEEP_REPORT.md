# Case 1 throughput sweep (board-independent)

This is a small integer model, not a Vivado timing result.  It uses the
frozen 22-descriptor graph at 640x480 and a 100 MHz clock (15 fps budget:
6,666,666 cycles/frame).  Run it with:

```text
python case1/model/throughput_sweep.py
python case1/model/test_throughput_sweep.py
```

The checked-in compact data is in
`case1/model/throughput_sweep.json`; the full per-stage details are available
from `throughput_sweep.py --json` without creating a simulation directory.

## Results

| scenario | external read words/frame | write words/frame | AXI read/write beats | effective outstanding | shared/graph cycles | optimistic fps |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| single-beat, legacy shared engine | 17,376,000 | 4,012,800 | 17,376,000 / 4,012,800 | 1 | 39,571,200 (memory 85,555,200) | 1.1688 |
| 3-row cache + pack2 + burst16, serial fabric | 4,819,200 | 4,012,800 | 2,409,600 / 2,006,400 | 1 | 39,571,200 | 2.5271 |
| same traffic + real 16-outstanding fabric | 4,819,200 | 4,012,800 | 2,409,600 / 2,006,400 | 16 | 39,571,200 | 2.5271 |
| same traffic + dot II=1, DW prefetch=1 | 4,819,200 | 4,012,800 | 2,409,600 / 2,006,400 | 16 | 21,715,200 | 4.6051 |
| two groups, II=1, narrow egress | 4,819,200 | 4,012,800 | 2,409,600 / 2,006,400 | 16 | 17,510,400 | 5.7109 |
| two groups, II=1, wide egress | 4,819,200 | 4,012,800 | 2,409,600 / 2,006,400 | 16 | 16,550,400 | 6.0422 |
| dedicated 80-lane streaming lower bound | 4,819,200 | 4,012,800 | 2,409,600 / 2,006,400 | 16 | 5,898,240 | 16.9542 |

The final row is the only row below the 15 fps budget, and it assumes a
dedicated, overlapped graph.  It is not achievable by simply instantiating a
second output-group core in the current sequential FSM.

The project specification reserves 10% of the frame for fill/drain and
control, i.e. a preferred compute ceiling of 6,000,000 cycles/frame.  The
dedicated 80-lane lower bound is 5,898,240 cycles, leaving only 101,760 cycles
of headroom before any real FIFO, weight-bank, CDC or DDR penalties.

## Interpretation for the next RTL step

* The uncached graph generates 17.376 M stage-read words and 4.0128 M write
  words per frame.  A three-row cache removes most 3x3 tap refills, but does
  not remove residual/upsample/output reads or any writes.  After ideal pack2,
  writes are still 45.43% of the AXI payload beats, so write-channel QoS and
  buffering must be designed explicitly.  A boardless write-burst leaf and an
  ID-less multi-descriptor write arbiter now exist as optional seams; the
  legacy serial top is intentionally unchanged until the board-side memory
  contract is fixed.
* A leaf `MAX_OUTSTANDING` queue has no effect when the shared AXI arbiter
  allows only one burst owner.  The `effective_outstanding=1` row models that
  case; `=16` is only valid after adding a fabric-level AR/R descriptor queue
  (or AXI IDs with ordered retirement).
  In this model, a cached packed frame's channel-overlap memory bound drops
  from 17,664,000 cycles (serial fabric) to 2,560,200 cycles (real 16-way
  fabric), but the shared compute FSM still dominates at 39,571,200 cycles.
* Packing is highly sensitive to launch order.  With the measured 4x4 trace
  pair rate (18.2593% of adjacent gaps), the cached traffic model is
  128,410,720 B/frame and 4,379,224 read beats; ideal pair formation would be
  70,656,000 B/frame and 2,409,600 read beats.  The trace is directional
  evidence only, but it shows why a FIFO behind a one-request-at-a-time
  adapter cannot assume a 2:1 reduction.
* The shared engine's visible collect/prefetch/egress sequencing dominates:
  reducing dot II and DW weight prefetch helps, but even two groups with a
  wide output cannot approach the 6.667 M-cycle budget.  Inter-stage and
  inter-pixel overlap (or a dedicated multirate graph) is the architectural
  change required for 15 fps.
* `arithmetic_lower_bound_cycles` is a MAC-count lower bound only.  It excludes
  weight-bank conflicts, cache refill latency, requant/activation, FIFO stalls,
  descriptor setup/parameter-repack overhead, DDR refresh and timing closure.
  Treat the 16.95 fps row as headroom for implementation, not as a board
  prediction.

## Fabric outstanding sizing (memory-only bound)

The following sweep isolates the AXI packing/burst/cache fabric from the CNN
compute FSM.  It uses the ideal cached pack2 traffic, 16-beat bursts, four
cycles of response latency, and independent read/write channel overlap.  The
table is generated by `model/throughput_sweep.py` and is also stored in
`model/throughput_sweep.json`.

| requested/effective outstanding | AXI beats/frame | channel-overlap memory cycles | memory-only fps | meets 15 fps? |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|:---:|
| 1 | 4,416,000 | 9,638,400 | 10.3752 | no |
| 2 | 4,416,000 | 4,819,200 | 20.7503 | yes |
| 4 | 4,416,000 | 2,560,200 | 39.0594 | yes |
| 8 | 4,416,000 | 2,560,200 | 39.0594 | yes |
| 16 | 4,416,000 | 2,560,200 | 39.0594 | yes |
| 32 | 4,416,000 | 2,560,200 | 39.0594 | yes |

This gives a concrete lower bound for the next integration step: two
effective fabric-level transactions already hide the assumed memory latency,
while four or more are issue-limited in this model.  It does **not** imply
15 fps for the current sequential CNN FSM; the compute rows above remain the
dominant blocker.  The read wrapper has a two-client, ordered-response seam,
and the write side has a multi-descriptor arbiter, so the next task is to
connect them behind bounded cache/producer FIFOs and measure real backpressure
rather than increasing leaf queue depths blindly.

## Parallel-bank proxy resource check

The standalone `c1_dot8x8_requant_bank` was synthesized at 100 MHz on an
Artix-7 proxy part.  The compact machine-readable result is
`case1/model/dot8x8_bank_proxy_synth_summary.json`.

To repeat one variant without binding Vivado to the interactive job:

```text
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File case1/scripts/run_dot8x8_bank_proxy_synth_detached.ps1 -Lanes 2
```

The launcher reports status under `case1/logs/dot8x8_bank_proxy_synth_runs/`
and deletes its private `.tmp_dot8x8_bank_proxy_synth_*` tree on completion.

| output-group lanes | LUT | FF | DSP | BRAM tiles | WNS (ns) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 8,144 | 2,437 | 16 | 0 | +3.479 |
| 2 | 16,576 | 4,874 | 32 | 0 | +2.260 |
| 4 | 33,148 | 9,748 | 64 | 0 | +2.266 |

The near-linear DSP/LUT scaling is expected: each lane contains a complete
64-product dot path plus requantization.  On the Ti60 budget, four lanes would
consume roughly 64 of 160 DSPs before DMA, caches and control logic; the
remaining throughput gap is therefore a scheduling/streaming problem, not a
single-core multiplier shortage.

The corresponding shared-FSM lane sweep (same 640x480 graph, compute-only)
shows where replication saturates:

| groups launched together | dot II | DW prefetch | scalar MAC lanes | engine cycles | compute-only fps | residual FSM bubble cycles |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 2 | 9 | 64 | 39,571,200 | 2.5271 | 32,880,000 |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 64 | 21,715,200 | 4.6051 | 15,024,000 |
| 2 | 1 | 1 | 128 | 16,550,400 | 6.0422 | 13,204,800 |
| 4 | 1 | 1 | 256 | 14,880,000 | 6.7204 | 13,207,200 |
| 8 | 1 | 1 | 512 | 14,361,600 | 6.9630 | 13,525,200 |

Thus even an unrealistically wide shared bank remains below 15 fps: collect,
stage transitions and bypass/output work are serialized.  The bank is a useful
building block only when paired with an inter-stage streaming scheduler.
